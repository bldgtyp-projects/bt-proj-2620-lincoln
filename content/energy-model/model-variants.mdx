As shown in the results below, in order to assess the building performance we have tested the building in five distinct
configurations (variants):

1. **Code-Minimum:** A version which meets but does not exceed the <VarLink hrefKey="narrative.energy_code.code_base_url" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><Var k="narrative.energy_code.name" /> / <Var k="narrative.energy_code.zone" /></VarLink> code minimums for assembly
   R-values, window U-values, airtightness and equipment efficiencies. This variant assumes a typical residential
   "extract-only" (bath fan) ventilation system, and that the building uses all-electric appliances and equipment for
   heating, cooling, hot-water and cooking.
2. **Passive House Assemblies:** This variant improves the assembly R-values to align with typical EnerPHit levels of performance.
3. **Improved Airtightness + ERV:** This variant improves whole-building airtightness to align with the EnerPHit standard (≤1.0ACH50) and substitutes the exhaust-only ventilation system with a balanced fresh air ventilation system with heat and moisture recovery (ERV).
4. **Passive House Windows \[PHI EnerPHit 'By Demand']:** A version which increases window U-values to meet the PHI-EnerPHit 'by Demand' energy performance and thermal comfort certification criteria. This is our recommended version.
