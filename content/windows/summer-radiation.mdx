* Even taking into account the street trees, fairly high solar radiation is observed along the
  south facade's upper floors which will reduce thermal comfort.
* The highest radiation levels are apparent on the upper floors, which receive the least amount of shading from the neighboring buildings or street trees.
* Coupled to the inevitable internal temperature stratification, the upper level
  the area most likely to see low thermal comfort in summer and early fall. 
* West-facing bulkhead glazing presents a major overheating risk. Consider external shading elements such as deciduous plantings, trellis structures, or exterior-mounted operable shades.
* Specify glazing with low SHGC (g-Value) for **all** south- and west-facing glazing.
