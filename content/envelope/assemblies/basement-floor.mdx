* Primary air-barrier to be [Intello Plus](https://475.supply/products/intello-plus) air and moisture vapor-retarder
  installed along underside of all floor joists. If preferred, substitute A/C plywood. Tape all seams to ensure air and
  moisture-vapor tight.
* Install mineral fiber batt or dense-packed cellulose insulation within framing cavity.
* Use service cavity / drop-ceiling below air/vapor retarder layer for all plumbing, electrical, and / or lighting fixtures. Install this layer prior to running any services in mechanical room.
